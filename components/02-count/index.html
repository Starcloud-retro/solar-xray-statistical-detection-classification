WHAT DOES “10,078” ACTUALLY MEAN?

Think in minutes first, not events.

At approximately one measurement per minute, seven days naturally produces around ten thousand timestamped observations. The detector later decides whether groups of unusual consecutive minutes should be treated as events.

7 days × 24 hours × 60 minutes ≈ 10,080 minute positions
10,078 actual rows ≈ 99.98% of minute positions represented
Each tiny mark = one CSV row 0 / 10,078
The marks accumulate by time. They represent observations, not detected flares.
ROWS TOUCHING EACH CALENDAR DATE

Why are there parts of eight dates if the study is only about seven days long?

Because the window begins partway through 4 September and ends partway through 11 September. It spans roughly seven elapsed days while touching eight calendar dates.
